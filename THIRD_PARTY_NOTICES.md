# Japanese reading lexicon attribution

The preview-only `japanese-reading.js` contains compact derived Japanese reading hints from general dictionaries, not user-supplied product names.

- **sph-mn/nihongo**, `data/jouyou-kanji-only-words.csv` and `data/jouyou-kanji.csv` (Japanese vocabulary and common-use kanji readings): https://github.com/sph-mn/nihongo — Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0), based in part on JMdict.
- **sepTN/kanji-data**, `data/words/chunk-9c.json` (Japanese word readings, including non-jōyō characters): https://github.com/sepTN/kanji-data — MIT-licensed software, underlying lexical data derived from JMdict/KANJIDIC2 where applicable. Generated reading hints are used only for on-device search suggestions and ranking, not as authority for an automatic identity match. The resulting lexical data is provided under CC BY-SA 4.0 as required for adapted CC BY-SA source material.
